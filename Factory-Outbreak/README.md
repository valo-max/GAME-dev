# Factory Outbreak

**Third-Person Zombie Shooter | Unity | C#**

A third-person combat prototype set inside an abandoned industrial environment. The player fights through waves of enemies before facing a dedicated boss encounter.

This project pushed me further into gameplay programming, AI behaviour, animation systems, and visual presentation.

---

## Gameplay Systems
* **Third-Person Shooting:** Over-the-shoulder aiming and accurate raycast firing mechanics.
* **Weapon & Ammunition:** Full weapon system (`AK-47`) with magazine capacity, reload loop, and infinite reserve ammo.
* **Wave Progression:** Staged enemy encounters inside an industrial warehouse environment.
* **Boss Encounter:** Nighttime arena showdown against a massive mutated boss.
* **Player Health & Damage:** Health tracking, screen hit indicators, and checkpoint-based respawn.

---

## AI & Combat Systems
* **Unity NavMesh:** Dynamic enemy pathfinding and obstacle avoidance.
* **Detection & Pursuit:** Target perception, aggro distance, and responsive player chasing.
* **Damage & Feedback:** Enemy health bars, bullet impact hit effects, and death ragdoll/animations.
* **Boss Behaviors:** Multi-phase attack patterns, telegraphing, and a dedicated boss health UI.

---

## Animation & Presentation
* Animator state machines and multi-directional Blend Trees.
* Mixamo character and enemy animation integration.
* Custom muzzle flashes, bullet sparks, and environmental lighting.
* Atmospheric industrial 3D environment composition.
